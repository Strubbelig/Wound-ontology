**Can a quality inhere in a wound? Yes.** WONT defines Wound as "a pathological disorder that is the output of a wound development process" and puts it under MILD's *pathological disorder* (`WONT_0000380`). In the OGMS tradition MILD follows, a disorder is a **material entity**: the physically altered tissue itself. BFO lets a quality inhere in any independent continuant, and a material entity is the standard case. So "Wound Area inheres in Wound" and "Wound Depth inheres in Wound" in your queue are well-formed. The burn rows ("Burn Size inheres in Thermal wound" and the others) are well-formed for the same reason.

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`
- **Wound vs. the process:** a quality can't inhere in a *process* such as wound healing or wound development. That's the one way these rows could go wrong. Here every value is the disorder class, so each one fits.
- **Even a hole works:** under BFO 2020, a quality can inhere in an immaterial entity too, such as a cavity or site. Only spatial regions are ruled out. So the answer would still be yes if someone modelled the wound bed as a site.
- **Possible tension:** "area" and "depth" come from PATO as kinds of size, which PATO usually puts on a physical object. A wound read as damaged tissue has a size, so this holds up. Whether "Wound Area" is the surface of the defect or the tissue around it is still your call for the thesis text.
`─────────────────────────────────────────────────`

**What's the reverse of "has quality"?** Yes, it's **"quality of"**:

| Direction | Relation | ID |
|---|---|---|
| bearer → quality | has quality | `RO_0000086` |
| quality → bearer | **quality of** | `RO_0000080` |

One detail matters for your queue: "quality of" is a narrower form of the general **"inheres in"**. Your queue's "inheres in" rows use the BFO 2020 version, `BFO_0000197`, and its reverse is **"bearer of"** (`BFO_0000196`). So:

- "Wound Area **inheres in** Wound" reverses to "Wound **bearer of** Wound Area".
- "Alginate **has quality** Material Biocompatibility" reverses to "Material Biocompatibility **quality of** Alginate".

Both pairs say the same thing about the world. They're just the general and the quality-only forms of the same link.
